# Roadmap

## v1.9.0 (Planned)

- **Draw types** — different ways to select cards from the deck (e.g. pulling randomly vs. sequentially working through a prepared deck)
- **Shuffle styles** — different shuffling algorithms or flavors (overhand, riffle, Hindu) affecting how the deck is prepared

## Future

- Date range statistics — draws per day view in history modal
- Streak tracking — consecutive days of practice
